# De voetbalvrouwen van PSV zijn kampioen van Nederland.

**EN**: PSV's women's football team are champions of the Netherlands.

**EN**: The PSV women's football team have won the Dutch league title for the first time in the club's history. They beat ADO Den Haag 2-0, taking the lead after three minutes, and can no longer be caught by other clubs. It was a double celebration in Eindhoven.
