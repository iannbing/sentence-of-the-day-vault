# Amnesty International telde in 2025 wereldwijd een recordaantal van 2707 executies.

**EN**: Amnesty International counted a record number of 2,707 executions worldwide in 2025.

## Summary

**EN**: Amnesty International's annual report recorded at least 2,707 executions in 17 countries in 2025 — the highest figure since the organisation began tracking in 1981, and a 78 percent rise on 2024. Iran alone accounted for 2,159 of those executions, double the previous year. Amnesty says a small group of governments uses the death penalty to spread fear and suppress dissent.
**NL-NL**: Amnesty International registreerde in 2025 minstens 2707 executies in zeventien landen, het hoogste aantal ooit. Dat is 78 procent meer dan in 2024. Iran voerde 2159 van die executies uit, een verdubbeling ten opzichte van het jaar daarvoor.

## Explanation

**EN**: The phrase 'een recordaantal van 2707' uses 'van' to introduce the specific figure — a standard Dutch journalistic construction: 'een aantal van', 'een bedrag van', 'een percentage van'. Read it as 'a record number, namely 2707'.
