# Premier Jetten ging naar het ziekenhuis na een allergische reactie.

**EN**: Prime Minister Jetten went to hospital after an allergic reaction.

## Summary

**EN**: Dutch Prime Minister Rob Jetten was stung by something while swimming in the sea near Bonaire. His body reacted badly, so he went to hospital. He was allowed to leave again after a few hours and shared a photo with the hospital staff on social media to say he was fine.
