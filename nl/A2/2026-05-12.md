# Gelukkig waren er genoeg mensen in de buurt die het beestje wilden helpen.

**EN**: Luckily, there were enough people nearby who wanted to help the little animal.

## Explanation

**EN**: The closing line of a short rescue story about a dog that fell into the Seine river in Paris and could not climb out on its own. The word 'gelukkig' signals relief — the story has a happy ending because bystanders were nearby and stepped in.
