# Magyar won vorige maand de verkiezingen van Viktor Orbán, die zestien jaar aan de macht was.

**EN**: Magyar won the election last month against Viktor Orbán, who had been in power for sixteen years.

## Explanation

**EN**: Hungary's new prime minister Péter Magyar defeated long-ruling Viktor Orbán last month. 'Aan de macht zijn' means to hold political power — a common phrase in news about governments and leaders.
